# Choosing Credits

A short, practical guide to picking the right programs without wasting weeks on applications.

## Start with the big three cloud credits

For most early startups, cloud credits are the highest-leverage dollar-per-hour application you will ever file. Apply to **AWS Activate**, **Google Cloud Startup Program**, and **Microsoft for Startups Founders Hub** in parallel — they are not mutually exclusive, and approval usually takes days to weeks. Credits typically expire (commonly 1–2 years), so **apply when you are ready to spend**, not a year early.

## AI credits: stack the provider offers

If you build on models, apply to the startup programs of the providers you actually use — **Anthropic**, **OpenAI**, **Cohere**, **Mistral**, **Together AI**, **Groq**, and **Cerebras** all run startup offers. These are often gated through accelerators, VCs, or partner programs, so ask your investors which partnerships they hold before applying cold.

## Software credits: audit your stack first

Before applying, list every paid tool you use (analytics, error tracking, CRM, communication). Startup programs from **HubSpot**, **Segment**, **Datadog**, **Sentry**, **Notion**, **Slack**, **Intercom**, and others commonly give 1 year free or heavy discounts to eligible startups — often found via your accelerator's partner benefits page. `verified` flags in [data/startup-credits.json](../data/startup-credits.json) mark which amounts were personally checked on the official page.

## Grants: non-dilutive, but competitive

Cash grants (**Hello Alice**, **Freed Fellowship Grant**, **Awesome Foundation**, the **Amber Grant**, etc.) are free money — no equity — but amounts are small and competition is real. Treat them as a bonus line item, not a funding strategy. Accelerator funding (**Y Combinator**, **Techstars**, **PearX**, **Antler**, **500 Global**) is the opposite: larger checks, but equity and a cohort commitment.

## Rules of thumb

1. **Read the expiry date.** Credits you can't use before they expire are worth $0. Map credits to your actual burn plan.
2. **Eligibility is narrow and enforced.** Most programs require: founded within the last few years, pre-Series B, a real product (or at least a live site), and sometimes VC backing. Don't fudge this — providers audit.
3. **Stack, don't double-spend.** You can hold credits from multiple clouds, but using three clouds in production is an operational cost. Park the extras.
4. **Re-check amounts before applying.** Figures change constantly; entries flagged `verified` were checked on the date shown, everything else is a lead to confirm.
