# Glossary

- **Startup credits** — dollar-denominated balances a provider grants a startup, spendable on that provider's services (cloud, APIs, SaaS). Not cash; usually non-transferable and expiring.
- **Activate** — AWS's startup credit program; tiered by affiliation (self-serve vs accelerator/VC-backed).
- **Founders Hub** — Microsoft's startup program bundling Azure credits with dev tools (GitHub, Microsoft 365) and support.
- **Non-dilutive** — funding that takes no equity: grants, credits, some competitions. Contrast with accelerator investments.
- **Partner-gated** — a program tier or offer accessible only through a partner (VC, accelerator, or platform partner) rather than open application.
- **Burn / runway** — how fast a startup spends cash, and how many months of operation the cash lasts. Credits extend runway by replacing cash spend.
- **Micro-grant** — a small cash grant (typically $500–$10,000) awarded by foundations or companies, usually monthly or rolling.
- **Cohort** — the group of startups going through an accelerator program together; many credit tiers are cohort-linked.
- **Sunsetting** — a program being wound down; listed for historical reference, not for new applications.
