> Startup founders can access **thousands of dollars in cloud, AI, and software credits** — plus non-dilutive cash grants — to extend their runway without giving up equity. This list tracks every notable program as of **September 2026**, with amounts quoted from the official program page where shown, and honest flags where they weren't.

A machine-readable copy lives in [`data/startup-credits.json`](data/startup-credits.json).

**Honesty flags:** `(verified)` = the official program page was personally fetched and the terms read on the date recorded in `verified_date` · `(unverified)` = the terms could not be confirmed firsthand — treat amounts as leads, not facts, and check the program page before applying.

**Status tags:** `(sunsetting)` = being wound down — avoid for new work · `(legacy)` = superseded but still around · `(acquired by X)` = product continues under new owner

## Cloud & Infrastructure Credits

Cloud and infrastructure credits — the highest-leverage dollars in the list. Apply to several in parallel; credits expire, so time applications to real spend.

- [Akamai Cloud Rise Start-Up Program](https://www.akamai.com/cloud/start-ups/rise) `(verified)` — **Up to $120,000 in Akamai Cloud credits (first year of a three-year program)** — First-year cloud credits followed by discounted (not free) services in years two and three.
- [Alibaba Cloud AI Catalyst Program](https://www.alibabacloud.com/en/startup/ai) `(verified)` — **Up to $120,000 cloud credits + up to 2B Model Studio tokens** — Alibaba Cloud credits, Model Studio tokens, POC coupons, AI expert office hours, and 12 months of Cloud Academy access.
- [AWS Activate](https://aws.amazon.com/startups/credits/) `(verified)` — **Up to $200,000 in AWS Activate Credits (Founders-path allocation not published)** — Tiered AWS credits plus technical support, training, and partner offers for startups.
- [Cloudflare Startup Program](https://www.cloudflare.com/startups/) `(verified)` — **Tiered: $10K / $100K / $350K (one year of credits)** — One year of tiered Cloudflare credits for security, performance, and edge services.
- [Databricks for Startups](https://www.databricks.com/product/startups) `(verified)` — **Startup credits offered — amount not publicly stated** — Databricks startup credits plus the separate consolidated Neon offer for venture-backed teams.
- [DigitalOcean Hatch](https://www.digitalocean.com/hatch) `(verified)` — **12 months of credits (amount varies; ~$10K/month cap) + 15 months Standard-tier support** — Cloud credits and extended support for early-stage startups, with an AI-native focus.
- [Google for Startups Cloud Program](https://cloud.google.com/startup) `(verified)` — **$2K (pre-funded) / $200K (Seed-Series A) / up to $350K (AI-first startups)** — Tiered Google Cloud credits with an elevated tier for AI-first startups, including Gemini/Vertex AI and TPU/GPU access.
- [Microsoft for Startups Founders Hub](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/startups) `(verified)` — **Up to $150K in startup credits; up to $200K for Investor Network-backed startups** — Azure credits unlocked as progress, service adoption, and sustained usage are demonstrated, plus dev tools and support.
- [MongoDB for Startups](https://www.mongodb.com/solutions/startups) `(verified)` — **Credits offered — amount not publicly stated** — Atlas credits for startups with an active website and LinkedIn presence.
- [Nebius for Startups / AI Lift](https://nebius.com/startups) `(verified)` — **AI Lift path: up to $150K cloud credits + $10K inference credits (for eligible NVIDIA Inception members)** — Selective GPU-cloud credit program with an AI Lift path for NVIDIA Inception members.
- [Neon Startup Program (Databricks)](https://neon.com/startups) `(verified)` — **Self-funded: up to $1K · Venture-backed: up to $200K combined Neon + Databricks** — Serverless Postgres credits, with a consolidated venture-backed tier spanning Neon and Databricks.
- [Netlify for Startups](https://www.netlify.com/partners/startups/) `(verified)` — **Discounted rate relative to standard pricing (amount not published)** — Partner-gated pricing discount for AI-native startup teams.
- [OVHcloud Startup Program](https://startup.ovhcloud.com/en/) `(verified)` — **Start: EUR 10,000 · Scale: up to EUR 100,000 (12 months)** — European cloud credits with 1-on-1 engineering time for startups and scaleups.
- [Railway Startup Program](https://railway.com/changelog/2024-04-26-startup-program) `(verified)` — **$5,000 in Railway credits (first rollout)** — Credits for deployment infrastructure, announced via the official changelog.
- [Scaleway Startup Program](https://www.scaleway.com/en/startup-program/) `(verified)` — **Founders: up to EUR 1,000 (1 yr) · Early Stage: EUR 9,000 (6 mo) · Growth Stage: EUR 36,000 (12 mo)** — Staged European cloud credits reviewed weekly by committee.
- [Vercel for Startups](https://vercel.com/startups) `(verified)` — **Up to $30,000 in Flexible Commitment Amount** — Commitment-based credits plus Enterprise-tier access and startup support.
- [Vultr Digital Startup Program](https://discover.vultr.com/startup-program) `(unverified)` — **Reported by third parties as up to $100,000 + up to 35% long-term discounts — not confirmed** — Startup credit program with reported long-term discount tiers.

## AI & API Credits

Model and AI-infrastructure credits. Several run official programs but publish no figures — amounts are negotiated per applicant, usually through VC/accelerator partners.

- [Anthropic — Claude for Startups](https://claude.com/programs/startups) `(verified)` — **Undisclosed — not published on the official page** — Free Claude API credits and priority rate limits for early-stage startups; 'token max to product market fit on us'.
- [Anyscale Startup Program](https://www.anyscale.com/startup) `(verified)` — **Up to $20K in Anyscale credits** — Ray-based compute credits plus dedicated field engineers for architecture design and hands-on support.
- [AssemblyAI Startup Program](https://www.assemblyai.com/contact/startup-program) `(verified)` — **12 months of recurring free credits (amount not published)** — A year of recurring voice-AI API credits: speech-to-text, streaming speech-to-text, and dictation APIs.
- [Baseten AI Startup Program](https://www.baseten.co/startup-program/) `(verified)` — **Up to $25K (Dedicated Inference/Training) + $2.5K (Model APIs)** — Up to $27.5K combined credits plus priority support, exclusive events, and GTM support for AI-first startups.
- [Cohere Startup Program](https://cohere.com/startup-program) `(unverified)` — **Unknown — no figure published on current pages** — Startup program announced June 2024 for early-stage companies using AI.
- [Deepgram for Startups](https://deepgram.com/startup-program) `(verified)` — **Up to $100K in credits, usable within 12 months** — Up to $100K in credits for speech-to-text, text-to-speech, and Voice Agent APIs.
- [ElevenLabs Grants](https://elevenlabs.io/grants) `(unverified)` — **Reported as 12 months of the Scale plan (33M characters) — not officially confirmed** — Startup grants covering ElevenLabs voice AI usage, reportedly a year of the Scale plan.
- [Fireworks AI — Fireworks for Startups](https://fireworks.ai/startups) `(verified)` — **'Build credits' — amount not published on the official page** — Startup build credits covering inference, image generation, speech-to-text, embeddings, fine-tuning, and on-demand GPU deployments.

- [Mistral AI — Free plan credits](https://mistral.ai/pricing) `(verified)` — **$10/month in API credits (Free plan)** — $10/month of API credits for anyone on the Free plan — a general free-tier benefit, not startup-specific.
- [NVIDIA Inception](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/startups/) `(verified)` — **No fixed amount — free membership with partner credit offers** — Free membership for AI startups: technical training, preferred hardware/software pricing, and partner offers including free cloud credits (amounts vary by partner).
- [OpenAI for Startups](https://openai.com/business/why-openai/startups/) `(verified)` — **Undisclosed — not published on the official page** — API credits and dedicated technical/GTM support unlocked through eligible VC partners.
- [Perplexity for Startups](https://www.perplexity.ai/startups) `(unverified)` — **Reported as $5K in Sonar API credits + 6 months Enterprise Pro — not officially confirmed** — Startup offer combining Sonar API credits with Enterprise Pro seats.
- [Together AI Startup Accelerator](https://www.together.ai/startup-accelerator) `(verified)` — **Tiered: up to $15K / $30K / $50K in platform credits** — Tiered inference credits plus forward-deployed engineering hours and GTM support for AI-native startups.

## Cash Grants & Micro-Funding

Non-dilutive cash grants — real money, no equity, usually competitive.

- [Amber Grants (WomensNet)](https://ambergrantsforwomen.com/) `(verified)` — **3 x $10,000/month + $50,000 year-end grant** — Three monthly grants (general, startup, and rotating business-category) with an additional year-end award.
- [Awesome Foundation](https://www.awesomefoundation.org/en) `(verified)` — **$1,000/month** — Rolling no-strings-attached micro-grants awarded monthly by autonomous local and thematic chapters.
- [AWS IMAGINE Grant](https://aws.amazon.com/government-education/nonprofits/aws-imagine-grant-program/) `(verified)` — **Varies by track (per third-party reports: up to $200K + credits); amounts not on the official page** — Unrestricted cash plus AWS credits for registered nonprofits, across three award tracks.
- [Cartier Women's Initiative](https://cartierwomensinitiative.com/) `(unverified)` — **Reported as $100K / $60K / $30K per category (~$1.9M annually) — third-party reports only** — Annual international fellowship for women-led impact businesses with per-category cash awards, INSEAD executive program, and coaching.
- [Comcast RISE](https://www.comcastrise.com/) `(unverified)` — **Historically $5,000 + in-kind package — current status unclear (third-party only)** — Comcast's small-business grant + in-kind services program; the standalone site has been folded into Project UP.
- [FedEx Small Business Grant Contest](https://newsroom.fedex.com/newsroom/asia-english/fedex-celebrates-innovative-asia-pacific-startups-empowering-businesses-to-scale-through-its-small-business-grant-contest) `(verified)` — **2026 APAC pool: $69,000 ($30K winner + 3 x $13K)** — Non-dilutive small-business grant contest; the verified 2026 APAC cycle awarded a $30K winner and three $13K special mentions.
- [Freed Fellowship Grant](https://freedfellowship.com) `(verified)` — **$500/month + $2,500 year-end grant** — Monthly no-strings-attached cash grants; each monthly fellow is considered for a year-end grant.
- [Hello Alice — Allstate Main Street Grants](https://helloalice.com/grants/allstate-main-street-grants-program/) `(verified)` — **Up to 100 x $20,000** — Two-stage structure: an accelerator cohort of 250 owners, with up to 100 receiving $20K grants.
- [Hello Alice — Fund Her Future (H&R Block, 2026)](https://www.helloalice.com/opportunities/grants/h-r-block-fund-her-future-grant-2026) `(verified)` — **Up to 4 x $50,000 packages ($45K cash + $5K services)** — Non-dilutive grant packages from H&R Block's Fund Her Future via Hello Alice.
- [Hello Alice — Grant Platform](https://helloalice.com/funding/grants/) `(verified)` — **Varies by opportunity — matching platform** — Free platform matching U.S. small businesses with rotating corporate and nonprofit cash grant opportunities.
- [Hello Alice — Pilot Small Business Growth Fund](https://helloalice.com/grants/pilot-small-business-growth-fund/) `(verified)` — **1 x $50,000 + 2 x $25,000 + 15 x $10,000** — Non-dilutive growth-fund grants from Hello Alice's Pilot partnership.
- [IFW by Honeycomb Credit — Grant Marketplace](https://www.ifundwomen.com/grants) `(verified)` — **Varies — rotating corporate partner grants (no open cycles as of 2026-09-29)** — Standing universal application matched against rotating corporate-sponsored grants (Honeycomb, Visa, BOTOX Cosmetic, Angel City FC, Caress, and others).
- [Tory Burch Foundation — Fellows Program](https://www.toryburchfoundation.org/fellows/) `(verified)` — **No cash amount stated on the official 2026 page (fellowship + services)** — Year-long fellowship for women entrepreneurs: peer network, coaching, advisors, and education.
- [Visa Everywhere Initiative](https://usa.visa.com/) `(unverified)` — **Historically ~$100K global winner / $20K audience favorite (third-party reports only)** — Annual fintech/commerce pitch competition with non-dilutive cash prizes, run in regional waves.
- [Visa She's Next](https://usa.visa.com/) `(unverified)` — **Varies by country and partner (e.g. 5 x EUR 10,000 Ireland) — third-party reports only** — Non-dilutive grants for women entrepreneurs, run in country-specific cycles.

### Accelerator Investments (Equity — Not Grants)

Larger checks that take equity or convertible instruments. Listed because founders treat them as runway packages, but they are investments, not credits.

- [500 Global — Flagship Accelerator](https://500.co/accelerators) `(unverified)` — **Not published on the official page (third parties claim $150K for ~6%)** — Flagship accelerator with global founder programs; investment terms not published officially.
- [Antler (US)](https://antler.co/location/us) `(verified)` — **$500K-$1M initial commitment + $650K partner credits (VC investment, NOT a grant)** — Pre-seed VC investment with a residency of up to three months in NYC, San Francisco, or Austin.
- [Pear VC / PearX](https://pear.vc/pearX/) `(verified)` — **$500K-$2M checks (VC investment, NOT a grant)** — Seed-stage VC checks through a 12-week program for under 25 teams, with required SF Bay Area relocation.
- [Plug and Play (Accelerator)](https://www.plugandplaytechcenter.com/startups/) `(unverified)` — **No standard published check — equity-free batch participation (per own press release)** — Corporate-partner accelerator, equity-free for batch participation; pilot/POC opportunities with corporate partners.
- [Techstars](https://www.techstars.com/newsroom/investment-terms-2024) `(verified)` — **Up to $120,000 per company (equity/convertible investment, NOT a grant)** — $20K cash for 6% (Contingent Equity Agreement) plus an optional $100K convertible note; also $4M+ in partner perks.
- [Y Combinator (Standard Deal)](https://www.ycombinator.com/deal) `(verified)` — **$500,000 standard deal (equity investment, NOT a grant)** — $125K post-money SAFE for 7% plus $375K uncapped MFN SAFE — an equity investment, not a grant.

### Perks-Only Programs (No Funding)

- [On Deck](https://www.beondeck.com/) `(verified)` — **No cash funding — $800K+ in perks + community** — Intense one-week in-person founder experience in San Francisco with perks and a 3,000+ member platform. Explicitly non-dilutive.
